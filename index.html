INTERNET SECRET AGENCY

Welcome to ISA. The Internet Secret Agency operates covertly to protect national security, investigate cybercrime, and maintain intelligence operations across digital frontiers.

ABOUT ISA

The Internet Secret Agency is a covert organization dedicated to safeguarding national security through advanced intelligence gathering, cyber operations, and criminal investigations in the digital age.

MISSION

To identify, investigate, and neutralize threats to national security operating within cyberspace and digital networks. We operate with precision, discretion, and unwavering commitment to protecting our nation.

VISION

A secure digital infrastructure defended by elite intelligence operatives and cutting-edge forensic technology. We operate in the shadows to keep the light safe.

OPERATIONAL DIVISIONS

ISA operates through six specialized divisions, each with distinct expertise and operational focus:

CRIMINAL INVESTIGATION

Investigates high-level cybercrime, financial fraud, and digital conspiracy. Specializes in tracking threat actors, dismantling criminal networks, and building federal cases against dangerous elements operating online.

FORENSICS

Expert analysis of digital evidence, data recovery, and forensic investigation. Utilizes advanced technology to extract intelligence from devices, networks, and encrypted communications.

INTERNAL AFFAIRS

Maintains operational security and integrity of the agency. Investigates internal misconduct, verifies agent authenticity, and ensures compliance with classified protocols.

SPECIAL OPERATIONS

Conducts covert field operations, cyber warfare, and direct action missions. Highly classified unit operating at the highest levels of national security.

CYBER DIVISION

Defends critical infrastructure and conducts offensive cyber operations. Specializes in penetration testing, vulnerability assessment, and defensive countermeasures against state-sponsored threats.

INTELLIGENCE

Gathers, analyzes, and distributes intelligence across all operations. Monitors global digital threats, predicts attack patterns, and provides actionable intelligence to division commanders.
